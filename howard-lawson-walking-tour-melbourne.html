Howard Lawson

Glamour by the Yarra

Don't know anything about those over-the-top Hollywood flats along the river in South Yarra? Well you will now with a deep dive into the coolest architect you never knew you knew, Howard Lawson.

What you'll see

Howard Lawson was an architect and builder who, between the wars, put up a run of extravagant apartment buildings along the river in South Yarra: Spanish Mission, Moderne, terraced gardens, a pool, and names borrowed straight from Hollywood.

The walk starts at the Golden Elm on the corner of Punt Road and Alexandra Avenue and follows the flats along the river and up the hill, with the story of the man who built them and what has happened to the buildings since.

Names that come up along the way:

Who it suits

Anyone who has driven along Alexandra Avenue and wondered about those buildings. It's the longest of the walks at about 2.5 hours and there's an uphill section, so wear comfortable shoes.

Your guide is Nick Heydon, an architectural guide who runs all four Nick Walks Melbourne routes.

QuestionsAnswered by Nick

What is the Howard Lawson walking tour?

A 2.5-hour architecture walk through South Yarra with Nick Heydon, focused on Howard Lawson, the architect and builder behind the Hollywood-style flats along the Yarra. You'll see the buildings, hear how they came to be and find out what has happened to them since. $50 per person.

Who was Howard Lawson?

A Melbourne architect and builder working between the wars, best known for theatrical apartment blocks in South Yarra such as Beverley Hills. He mixed Spanish Mission and Moderne styles with gardens, a pool and a strong sense of showmanship. Nick calls him the coolest architect you never knew you knew.

How strenuous is it?

It's the longest of the walks at about 2.5 hours and includes an uphill section. The pace is unhurried, but wear comfortable shoes.

Where does the walk start?

At the Golden Elm on the corner of Punt Road and Alexandra Avenue, South Yarra.
