Train & Walk

South Yarra to Anzac Station

Who needs traffic? Catch the train to South Yarra and explore its dirty secrets, engrossing history and the very latest in architecture on this leafy two hour walk. We walk to the soaring cavern of Anzac Station and catch the train home.

What you'll see

South Yarra has some of the best residential architecture in Melbourne and a lot of history it would rather you didn't ask about. We start on the platform at South Yarra Station and head through the leafy streets: Arts and Crafts houses, Robin Boyd, Peter McIntyre, Wood Marsh and the very latest apartment buildings, along with a few walls, monuments and laneways with stories attached.

The walk finishes at Anzac Station, the new Metro Tunnel station on St Kilda Road, and you catch the train home from there.

Who it suits

Anyone who'd rather walk than drive. Two hours, a little over two kilometres, one uphill section, and a train at each end.

Your guide is Nick Heydon, an architectural guide who runs all four Nick Walks Melbourne routes.

QuestionsAnswered by Nick

Do I need a myki?

Yes. You'll need it to get to South Yarra Station and to catch the train home from Anzac Station at the end.

How far is the walk?

A little over two kilometres over two hours, with one uphill section. It's a leafy, unhurried walk.

Where does it finish?

At Anzac Station on St Kilda Road, the new Metro Tunnel station. Nick walks you in and you catch the train home from there.

How is this different from the Howard Lawson walk?

Both are in South Yarra. Howard Lawson is a deep dive on one architect and his flats along the river. Train & Walk covers the wider suburb: houses, apartment buildings, history and the new station, across many eras and architects.
