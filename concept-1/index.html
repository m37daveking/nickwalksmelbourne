Nick Walks Melbourne

Lunch loop · Train walk
See Melbourne like you've never seen it before.
Join filmmaker and architectural guide Nick Heydon.

Melbourne is one of the world's great cities.

I absolutely love exploring what makes it special.

Join me as we uncover extraordinary buildings, fascinating characters and the big ideas that shaped the city around us. You'll learn how to spot a Modernist masterpiece, understand the stories hidden within our streets and discover details you've probably walked past hundreds of times without noticing.

Once you know what you're looking at, the whole city comes alive.
