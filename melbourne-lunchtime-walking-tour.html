City Lunchtime Walk

See Melbourne in your lunch break

Step out of the office for a brisk 45-minute architectural adventure through the top end of town. Explore Australia’s first skyscraper, secret spaces and uncover some of the city’s most exciting new design.

Fast, fascinating and full of surprises, come on your own or as a work group.

The walkRoute 01

What you'll see

We meet on Spring Street, a couple of minutes from Parliament Station, and spend the whole 45 minutes in the top end of the city: the Paris end of Collins Street, the clubs and lanes around it, and the new buildings squeezed in between.

The mix runs from Victorian grandeur to Modernist confidence, a dose of Brutalism, and work finished in the last few years. You come out knowing what you're looking at and why it's there.

Names that come up along the way:

  • Parliament
  • Melbourne Club
  • Collins Place
  • Total House
  • Monaco House
  • Meyers Place
  • Waiter's Club
  • Lyceum Club
  • Reed & Barnes
  • 6 Degrees
  • Breathe

Who it suits

Anyone with 45 minutes. The walk was designed for people who work in the city and want to see it properly, and it works just as well as a team outing.

For a work group, book together on TryBooking or email Nick about a private walk for your team.

Your guide is Nick Heydon, an architectural guide who runs all four Nick Walks Melbourne routes.

QuestionsAnswered by Nick

Can I do a Melbourne walking tour in my lunch break?

Yes. The City Lunchtime Walk runs for 45 minutes, starts on Spring Street and stays in the top end of town, so you can be back at your desk within the hour. It costs $25 and you pick a date on TryBooking.

What will I see in 45 minutes?

More than you'd expect. Australia's first skyscraper, a few hidden spaces, the grand clubs of the Paris end and some of the best new buildings in the city. Nick keeps it moving.

Can we come as a work group?

Yes. Book as a group on TryBooking, or email Nick about a private walk for your team.

Where does the walk start?

At Spring Street Grocer, 157 Spring Street, a couple of minutes from Parliament Station. Arrive five minutes early so Nick can check you in.
